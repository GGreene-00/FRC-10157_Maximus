**The Recounting of Roman Robotics — FRC Team 10157**

Every robotics team begins with a spark — a moment where curiosity turns into action. For us, Roman Robotics, FRC Team 10157, that spark continues to grow as we find our place in the world of competitive robotics.

We are a team built on learning. From the very beginning, we came together as students and mentors ready to take on something new, even when we didn’t have all the answers. For us, it has never been about perfection—it’s about experimenting, improving, and building something meaningful together.

Last season, everything was new. We learned how to use tools, write code, and understand the flow of competition through hands-on experience. There were challenges that felt overwhelming and moments where things didn’t go as planned. But those experiences shaped who we are. Instead of giving up, we kept going—asking questions, testing ideas, and turning uncertainty into confidence.

Our robot, Maximus, represents that journey. It isn’t just a machine—it’s proof of what we can accomplish through teamwork and persistence. Every adjustment, every fix, and every success—no matter how small—reminds us that progress comes from learning by doing. Maximus reflects not just what we knew at the time, but our willingness to grow.

As a team, we continue to evolve. Members step into leadership roles, new students join and bring fresh ideas, and together we build on what we’ve learned before. Each season adds another layer to our experience, strengthening both our skills and our connection as a team.

Competing in the Mid-Atlantic District challenges and inspires us. Every event is an opportunity to improve, collaborate, and push ourselves further. We strive to embody gracious professionalism by supporting other teams while continuing to grow through every match and every obstacle.

What defines us isn’t how much experience we have—it’s our mindset. We believe that mistakes are part of the process, that questions lead to discovery, and that growth comes from trying again. We are constantly learning, constantly improving, and always looking ahead.

Our story is still being written. With every passing year, we are building not only better robots, but better thinkers, leaders, and innovators.

We are Roman Robotics — FRC Team 10157 — and we’re always moving forward.
